Solar Vehicle Project

Infotainment, telemetry, and race driving.

2021 - 2023 / University of Minnesota

I developed infotainment software and telemetry hardware for the Solar Vehicle Project, working across circuit design, software development, and system integration.

I also drove in the 2022 American Solar Challenge and created promotional films for the team. Freya won the Multi-Occupant Vehicle class that year, Minnesota's first American Solar Challenge road-race victory.

Electronics & vehicle operation

Circuit boards, cockpit, communications, and the tablet interface.

Software and electronics

My engineering work included the F1 infotainment application and G1 telemetry hardware. I contributed to existing systems as well as new designs, connecting the electronics, vehicle data, and interfaces used by the team.

On the road

Telemetry helps the support crew track speed, battery condition, and energy use. Driving in the American Solar Challenge gave me direct experience of the vehicle and the demands placed on its systems throughout a long-distance race.

Documenting the team

I filmed and developed promotional material alongside my engineering and driving responsibilities, helping show the vehicle, the build process, and the people behind it.
