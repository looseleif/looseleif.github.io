About Me

Hi, I’m Chase Anderson, an engineer working at the intersection of embedded systems, robotics, and machine intelligence. My background spans embedded security, robotic manipulation, hardware design, and systems integration, with projects ranging from secure communication platforms to robotics control systems.

I’ve contributed to projects in both research and industry settings, focusing on hands-on development, system-level design, and pushing prototypes into real-world use. Outside of technical work, I’ve worked with diverse teams, from startups to academic labs, to bring ideas from concept to implementation, often mentoring, collaborating, and learning along the way.
