Chase Anderson

Embedded hardware, firmware & robotics.

I develop hardware and firmware for tethered drones and secure computing systems. My work spans power distribution, communications, embedded security, and system validation.

At the University of Minnesota, I researched robotic perception and manipulation in the CHOICE Robotics Lab. My master's research extended self-supervised Q-learning for robotic mechanical search in clutter, with a simplified second platform and additional test tasks.

Outside that work, I build robotics, sensing, and software projects and document them through photos and video.
