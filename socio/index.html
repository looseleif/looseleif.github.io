THE WORK

Make the conversation easier to follow.

Who has spoken? What question is still open? When could someone else join in? remorse explores ways to make that context visible while a conversation unfolds.

The current footage shows live session text, speaker-presence displays, conversational prompts, and a mobile companion. Haptics and wearable feedback are an ongoing direction for the product.
