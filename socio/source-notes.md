# remorse: recording and transcript sources

The project is remorse. Socio is the company; IYKYD documents the work.

Searched the available English transcripts of all 22 public videos in the IYKYD Videos feed, from December 2024 through October 2026. Searches covered remorse, transcripts, speakers, software, interfaces, prototypes, haptics, wearables, and related terms. Inspected storyboards and the extracted demonstration frames before selecting footage. Auto-generated transcripts can contain recognition errors; summaries below paraphrase the recording and do not reproduce disputed bill numbers or treat UI metrics as validated measurements.
