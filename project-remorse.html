Desktop software / Phone & watch integration

remorse: Conversation Interfaces

Live transcription, speaker tracking, and haptic feedback.

I am developing remorse, a Socio Studios product, as a desktop hub for live conversations. The application brings together transcription, speaker tracking, questions, and session history.

Phone and watch clients connect to the hub, with support for haptic feedback. Optional local language-model tools support transcript retrieval.

THE SYSTEM

A conversation has more than one thread.

remorse keeps the transcript, speaker activity, and unanswered questions in one session. Phone and watch clients extend the desktop hub with companion views and haptic feedback.

DESKTOP HUB

Follow the conversation

Live transcript, speaker ledger, current statements, and questions that can be revisited.

PHONE & WATCH

Carry the context

Companion clients connect to the session, with haptic routing handled by the hub.

SESSION HISTORY

Return to the thread

Saved workspaces and conversation archives support explicit review. Optional retrieval tools are controlled per workspace.
