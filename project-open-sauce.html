VOLUNTEERING / SYNC TANK PRESENTATIONS

Open Sauce

Demonstrating Sync Tank.

My volunteering and presentations at Open Sauce have mainly focused on Sync Tank. I use the system to demonstrate interactive robotics, camera networking, and real-time embedded processing.

The aquarium gives visitors a physical setting for seeing how cameras, connected hardware, and live software work together.

OBJECTIVES

Show how the system works.

  • Make interactive robotics tangible through the Sync Tank demonstration.
  • Explain how networked cameras bring live views into the system.
  • Connect the visible behavior to the embedded processing behind it.

PRESENTATION / SYNC TANK

Three parts of one demonstration.

Interactive robotics

Present the physical system and show how interaction connects to robotic hardware.

Camera networking

Show live camera views and explain how the cameras connect to the observation interface.

Real-time embedded processing

Explain the firmware and processing that connect sensing, communication, and hardware response.
